# Item Atlas

Where an OSRS item comes from and what it's used for, as shown in the RuneLite side panel.

## Language

**Source**:
One way to obtain an item: a recipe, a drop, a shop or a spawn.
_Avoid_: Origin, provider

**Drop source**:
The monster or object a drop comes from, optionally narrowed by a drop version.

**Drop version**:
The wiki's variant of a drop source, written after `#` in the wiki key ("Bronze dragon#Standard") and shown in brackets ("Bronze dragon (Standard)").
_Avoid_: Variant, form

**Location**:
A named place where a source can be found, which the route button can target.

**Required item**:
An item a location needs before its source can be looted, such as a chest's key.
_Avoid_: Key (when the item isn't a key), unlock item

**Key status**:
Where the player keeps a required item: carried, in bank, in group storage, not owned, or not yet checked.
